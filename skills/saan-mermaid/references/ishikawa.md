# ishikawa-beta

Header: `ishikawa-beta`. Since 11.13 (beta). Example: `assets/examples/fishbone.mmd`.

Saan use: causes behind one outcome.

## Syntax

- The first indented line is the outcome (the fish head).
- Each category is one indent deeper; causes one more indent below their category.

## Pitfalls

- Indentation defines the tree. Mixing tabs and spaces breaks it.
- Hosts older than 11.13 cannot render it. Check with `--target`, or use a nested list in Markdown.
