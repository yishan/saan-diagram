Saan Kits

Agent skills for turning requirements into diagrams, documents, and prototypes. Install each skill independently.

  • Saan Diagram

    Turn business descriptions, requirements, and whiteboard screenshots into consistent diagrams in English or Chinese.

    For presentations, external delivery, and Feishu/Lark whiteboards. Export HTML, SVG, and PNG.

    npx skills add yishan/saankits --skill saan-diagram
    Explore diagram types, examples, and dependencies

  • Saan Mermaid

    Draw with Mermaid and review business workflows against an independent control checklist.

    For diagrams in Markdown and design documents, and for approval-process reviews.

    npx skills add yishan/saankits --skill saan-mermaid
    Explore review rules, examples, and dependencies

  • Saan Discovery

    Agree the consumer, enterprise, public-sector, or mixed research scope, then explore evidence and product fit one topic at a time.

    Interview guides, topic-based briefs, and follow-up questions. Interaction and outputs follow your requested language.

    npx skills add yishan/saankits --skill saan-discovery
    Explore discovery workflows, examples, and installation

  • Saan PRD Writing

    Turn requirements and prototypes into full specifications or scoped changes with concrete conditions, fields, states, and outcomes.

    PRDs, acceptance scenarios, and open decisions, with English and Chinese templates and a worked example.

    npx skills add yishan/saankits --skill saan-prd-writing
    Explore document structure, examples, and installation

Planned: prototyping.

Install the collection

npx skills add yishan/saankits

Choose skills and target agents during installation. Install again to add skills released later. Each introduction lists its dependencies.
