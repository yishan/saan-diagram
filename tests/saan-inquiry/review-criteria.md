# Reviewer criteria

Review meaning and the supplied evidence, not exact headings or keywords. Freeze these criteria before inspecting the responses.

1. Volunteer turn 1: separates cancellations from the proposed motive; asks one consequential question or offers a useful tentative framing without a broad intake checklist.
2. Volunteer turn 2: respects “no more questions,” reuses the reported event, does not assign blame or generalize from one event, and proposes an actionable, bounded observation or trial with a review condition.
3. Volunteer turn 3: incorporates the changed result rather than asking again about the known event; distinguishes an intermediate improvement from the original goal; revises the insufficient explanation while leaving the cause of later failures unresolved where evidence is absent.
4. Across the dialogue: no invented user answers, causal certainty, event frequency, commitments, implementation, or external action. An optional proposal is not treated as already agreed or performed.
5. Personal plan: uses the stated time, supplies, and weekday scope, asks no follow-up questions, provides an achievable one-week plan and a useful review signal without a purchase or invented personal diagnosis.
6. Sentence task: outputs just a clearer sentence with no interview or unsupported measured impact.
7. Across cases: language and requested pace are respected; output is proportionate; a plan is not called verified; useful guidance is not withheld merely because information is incomplete.

Record any partial pass, failure, and revision. A passing text simulation does not establish host behavior, real-world effectiveness, or superiority over another method.
