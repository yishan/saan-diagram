**Request A**

Try five 15-minute sessions after dinner, using your paper and pencil. The aim this week is to see whether sketching fits your evenings and feels rewarding.

Each session: 1 minute choosing a subject, 12 minutes drawing, 2 minutes noting how it felt.

- **Monday:** Draw one nearby object, focusing on its outline.
- **Tuesday:** Draw the same object from another angle.
- **Wednesday:** Sketch your free hand.
- **Thursday:** Draw two objects together, noticing their relative sizes.
- **Friday:** Repeat whichever subject you enjoyed most.

Stop when the timer ends. If you miss a day, continue with the next session without catching up. No weekend session required.

After Friday, review your notes:

- **Keep it** if it fits your evenings and you feel some desire to draw again.
- **Adjust it** if drawing feels worthwhile but the routine feels burdensome; try five minutes or fewer days.
- **Pause it** if it consistently feels like another chore.

Judge the routine by enjoyment and ease of returning, rather than how polished the drawings look.
